# Example: Northstar Team Handbook

This fictional document is for testing Margin. It contains no real company data.

## Project review

The Northstar product review takes place on the first Tuesday of each month. Teams should submit agenda items by 17:00 on the preceding Friday. The review owner sends a written summary within two business days.

## Access requests

New project members request access through the team lead. The lead confirms the person's role before an administrator grants access. Temporary access expires after fourteen days.

## Release checklist

Before a release, the team checks automated tests, records a rollback plan, verifies the support contact, and updates the change log. The release owner makes the final decision to proceed.

## Budget

The annual training budget for this fictional team is 4,500 dollars. Spending requests above 500 dollars require approval from the department lead.
